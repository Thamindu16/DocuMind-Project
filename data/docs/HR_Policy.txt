TechNova Solutions - Employee Handbook & HR Policy

1. Working Hours and Remote Work
TechNova Solutions operates on a hybrid model. Employees are required to be in the office for a minimum of 3 days per week (Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday). The core working hours are from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Outside of these hours, employees may arrange their schedules as long as they complete 40 hours per week. Fully remote work is only permitted for specific engineering roles with prior approval from the Head of Department.

2. Annual Leave and Holidays
Full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave must be requested at least 14 days in advance through the HR portal. Unused annual leave can be carried over to the next year, up to a maximum of 5 days. Sick leave is separate and provides up to 10 paid days per year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days.

3. Expense Reimbursement
Employees can claim up to $500 annually for home office equipment (monitors, chairs, keyboards). Receipts must be submitted within 30 days of purchase. Travel expenses for client meetings are fully reimbursed, but economy class must be used for flights under 5 hours.

4. Code of Conduct
TechNova Solutions enforces a zero-tolerance policy against harassment and discrimination. Employees must maintain confidentiality regarding client data and proprietary code. Any conflict of interest must be disclosed to the HR department immediately.
