TechNova Solutions - Tier 1 Customer Support Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

1. Handling Incoming Tickets
All support tickets must be acknowledged within 15 minutes of creation. The standard greeting is: "Thank you for contacting TechNova Solutions. My name is [Agent Name], how can I assist you today?" Always maintain a professional and empathetic tone, even if the customer is frustrated.

2. Ticket Escalation Matrix
If an issue cannot be resolved within 30 minutes, it must be escalated to Tier 2 support.
Tier 1 handles: Password resets, basic billing inquiries, and UI navigation help.
Tier 2 handles: API integration errors, database connection issues, and software bugs.
Tier 3 handles: Server outages, security breaches, and core infrastructure failures.

3. Refund Policy
Customers are eligible for a full refund if they request it within 14 days of purchase. After 14 days, only a partial refund (50%) can be offered up to 30 days. Refunds are processed back to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.

4. Closing a Ticket
Before closing a ticket, always ask: "Is there anything else I can help you with today?" Update the ticket status to 'Resolved' and send a customer satisfaction survey link. Do not close a ticket unless the customer confirms the issue is fixed.
