TechNova Code of Conduct

Respectful Workplace: Harassment, bullying and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated. Employees who experience or witness such behaviour should report it to HR or through the anonymous ethics hotline. Every report is investigated within 10 working days.

Conflicts of Interest: Employees must declare any financial interest in a supplier, customer or competitor to their manager. Working for a competitor while employed by TechNova is not allowed.

Gifts: Gifts from suppliers or customers worth more than 10,000 LKR must be declined or handed over to HR. Cash gifts are never acceptable.

Confidential Information: Customer data and internal documents must not be shared outside the company without written approval. This duty continues after employment ends.
