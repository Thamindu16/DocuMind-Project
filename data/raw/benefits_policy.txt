TechNova Benefits Policy

Health Insurance: All full-time employees are covered by the company health insurance plan from their first day. The plan covers hospital stays up to 500,000 LKR per year and outpatient visits up to 75,000 LKR per year. Dependents can be added during the first 30 days of employment.

Training Budget: Every employee has an annual training budget of 100,000 LKR for courses, certifications and conferences. Requests must be approved by the line manager before payment, and certification exam fees are reimbursed after a pass result.

Home Office Allowance: Employees approved for remote work receive a one-time home office allowance of 40,000 LKR for a desk, chair or monitor. A monthly internet allowance of 3,000 LKR is also paid to them.

Performance Reviews: Performance is reviewed twice a year, in June and December. Each review includes a self-assessment, a manager assessment and a goal-setting conversation. Employees who exceed expectations are considered for promotion in the next cycle.
