TechNova Solutions HR Policy

Annual Leave: Every full-time employee is entitled to 14 days of paid annual leave per year. Leave must be requested at least 3 working days in advance through the HR portal. Unused leave can be carried over to the next year, up to a maximum of 5 days.

Remote Work: Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week with manager approval. Core working hours are 10 AM to 4 PM, and employees must be reachable during these hours.

Sick Leave: Employees receive 7 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 2 consecutive days.

Expense Claims: Work-related expenses must be submitted within 30 days with original receipts. Claims above 50,000 LKR need director approval.
