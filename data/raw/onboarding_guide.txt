TechNova Onboarding Guide

First Day: New employees collect their laptop and access card from the IT helpdesk at the start of their first day, followed by a welcome session with HR.

Probation: The probation period is six months. A review meeting is held with the manager at the end of the third month and again at the end of the probation period.

Buddy Programme: Each new employee is paired with a buddy from the same team for the first 60 days. The buddy answers day-to-day questions and helps the new employee settle in.

Mandatory Training: Within the first two weeks every new employee must complete the security awareness course and the code of conduct course.
